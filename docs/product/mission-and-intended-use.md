# Mission and intended use

**Mission.** Help people with low vision navigate their surroundings and, over time, build awareness of their environment.

**Intended use (proposed wording, under review: Q244 of the architecture questionnaire).**
MaxSight is an assistive environmental-awareness application designed to help people with low vision interpret their surroundings, identify potential mobility hazards, and receive contextual assistance while navigating indoor and outdoor environments. It is not intended to replace established mobility aids, professional orientation and mobility training, or independent safety judgment.

**Never claimed:** collision prevention, guaranteed detection, safe independent navigation, medical diagnosis or treatment, replacement of a white cane or guide dog.

See `docs/architecture/v1/` for the full specification.
