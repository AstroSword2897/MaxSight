# ADR-006: Detection, hazard assessment and assistance policy are separate responsibilities

- **Register ID:** DR-009
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Source:** Q2, Q26, directive

## Context / rationale
Hazard depends on context and the user, not just the class

## Alternatives considered
Learned hazard head

## Decision
Detection, hazard assessment and assistance policy are separate responsibilities

## Consequences
Rule-based assessor between detector and policy

## Dependencies
DR-010

## Validation required
Rule-trace audit (SG-05)
